Chimney FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a chimney be cleaned?

Chimneys should be inspected annually and cleaned as needed — typically once a year for regularly used fireplaces — to prevent creosote buildup and keep your fireplace safe.

What is creosote and why is it dangerous?

Creosote is a flammable, tar-like residue that builds up inside your chimney as wood burns. Too much creosote can ignite and cause a dangerous chimney fire, which is why regular cleaning is important.

What are the signs my chimney needs cleaning?

Signs include smoke backing up into the room, a strong odor from the fireplace, black flaky creosote buildup, or poor draft. It’s also smart to have it swept before first use each winter.

Do you inspect and maintain chimneys too?

Yes. Along with sweeping, we inspect the firebox, flue, cap, crown and damper, and handle minor masonry and cap maintenance.

How long does a chimney cleaning take?

Most chimney cleanings and inspections take about 1–2 hours depending on the condition and level of buildup.
