Air Duct FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

Most Georgia homes benefit from a professional air duct cleaning every 1–2 years, or sooner if you have pets, allergies, recent renovations, or notice more dust than usual.

How long does air duct cleaning take?

Most residential air duct cleanings take about 2–4 hours depending on the size of your home and the number of vents.

Does air duct cleaning help with allergies?

Yes. Removing dust, pollen, pet dander and other debris from your ductwork reduces the allergens recirculated through your home, which can ease allergy and asthma symptoms.

Do you seal and maintain ductwork?

Yes. Along with cleaning, we handle minor duct sealing and maintenance to fix leaks and improve airflow and efficiency.

Do I need to pay anything to book?

No prepayment is required to schedule. You’ll get upfront, honest pricing before any work begins — no hidden fees or surprises.

Which areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Georgia and the surrounding metro area, including Marietta, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Alpharetta, Roswell and more.
