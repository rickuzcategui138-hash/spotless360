Gutter FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should gutters be cleaned?

We recommend cleaning gutters at least twice a year, in spring and fall, and more often if your home is surrounded by trees, to prevent clogs and water damage.

What happens if I don’t clean my gutters?

Clogged gutters overflow and send water toward your roof, siding and foundation, which can cause leaks, rot, foundation damage, mold and pest problems.

Do you repair gutters and downspouts too?

Yes. Along with cleaning, we reseal leaks, refasten sagging gutters, clear and reattach downspouts, and can install gutter guards on request.

What are the signs my gutters need attention?

Signs include water overflowing the edges, sagging or pulling-away gutters, staining on siding or foundation, and plants growing in the gutters.

Do you install gutter guards?

Yes. We can install gutter guards on request to reduce debris buildup and cut down on how often your gutters need cleaning.

Which areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Atlanta and the surrounding metro area, including Marietta, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Alpharetta, Roswell and more.
