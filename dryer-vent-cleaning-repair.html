Dryer Vent FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

We recommend cleaning your dryer vent at least once a year, or more often for large households or long vent runs, to reduce fire risk and keep your dryer running efficiently.

What are the signs of a clogged dryer vent?

Common signs include clothes taking more than one cycle to dry, a very hot dryer or laundry room, a burning smell when the dryer runs, and excess lint around the vent.

Why is dryer vent cleaning important?

Lint buildup is a leading cause of home fires. Regular dryer vent cleaning reduces that risk, shortens drying times, and helps your dryer run more efficiently and last longer.

How long does dryer vent cleaning take?

Most dryer vent cleanings take about 45–90 minutes depending on the length and layout of the vent line.

Do you repair dryer vents too?

Yes. We reconnect and repair damaged vent lines, replace crushed or disconnected ducts, and can install pest guards on request.
