How Often Should You Clean Your Air Ducts in Georgia?

By Spotless360 · Jul 26, 2026 · 5 min read

If you’ve ever wiped down your furniture only to see a fresh layer of dust the next day, your air ducts may be the culprit. In a city like Georgia — with heavy pollen seasons, high humidity and plenty of pet-friendly homes — ductwork collects debris faster than most homeowners realize. So how often should you actually have it cleaned?

The general rule of thumb

For most Georgia homes, a professional air duct cleaning every 1 to 2 years keeps airflow healthy and dust under control. That’s a good baseline — but your ideal schedule depends on what’s happening inside your home.

When you should clean them more often

Consider cleaning your air ducts every 1 to 2 years if any of these apply to your household:

  • Pets. Dander and fur build up quickly in ductwork.
  • Allergies or asthma. Cleaner ducts mean fewer airborne triggers.
  • Recent renovations. Construction dust and drywall debris settle deep in the system.
  • Smokers in the home. Residue accumulates on duct surfaces over time.
  • A new home purchase. You never know when the ducts were last serviced.

Signs you shouldn’t wait for your next scheduled cleaning

Sometimes your home tells you it’s time. Book a cleaning sooner if you notice:

  • Visible dust puffing from the vents when the system kicks on
  • More dust settling on surfaces than usual
  • Musty or stale odors when the HVAC runs
  • Uneven airflow — some rooms hotter or cooler than others
  • Allergy symptoms that flare up indoors
Clean ducts don’t just cut down on dust — they help your HVAC run more efficiently, which can lower your energy bills.

What a professional cleaning includes

A thorough air duct service is more than running a vacuum through a vent. At Spotless360, our process covers:

  • Full supply and return duct cleaning
  • Vent and register cleaning
  • Powerful negative-air extraction to pull out debris
  • Optional sanitizing and deodorizing
  • Minor duct sealing and maintenance to improve airflow

The bottom line

Every 1–2 years is a smart baseline for most Georgia homes — but pets, allergies, renovations and the warning signs above may mean cleaning sooner. When in doubt, a quick inspection will tell you exactly where you stand.
