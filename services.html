Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions Atlanta homeowners ask us most.

How often should I have my air ducts cleaned?

Most Atlanta homes benefit from a professional air duct cleaning every 2–3 years, or sooner if you have pets, allergies, recent renovations, or notice more dust than usual.

Why is dryer vent cleaning so important?

Lint buildup is a leading cause of home fires. Regular dryer vent cleaning reduces that risk, shortens drying times, and helps your dryer run more efficiently.

How often does a chimney need to be cleaned?

Chimneys should be inspected annually and cleaned as needed — typically once a year for regularly used fireplaces — to remove creosote buildup and keep your fireplace safe.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

We recommend cleaning gutters at least twice a year, in spring and fall, and more often if your home is surrounded by trees, to prevent clogs and water damage.
